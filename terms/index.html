Program description

devtooligan Agent Messaging is a non-marketing SMS program operated under the devtooligan name. It sends user-requested agent responses, task-status updates, operational notifications, and account or security alerts to people who explicitly opt in.

Consent and opt-in

You may opt in by texting START to the program number provided directly by devtooligan. By opting in, you consent to receive recurring automated text messages related to the program. Consent is not a condition of purchasing any goods or services.

Message frequency and charges

Message frequency varies based on your requests and agent activity. Message and data rates may apply according to your mobile carrier and plan.

Opt-out and help

Reply STOP at any time to opt out. After an opt-out request, you may receive one confirmation message, and no further program messages will be sent unless you opt in again.

Reply HELP for help or visit github.com/devtooligan for support.

Delivery

Wireless carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Message delivery is subject to carrier availability and other systems outside the program's control.
