devtooligan Agent Messaging

Privacy policy

Last updated: August 25, 2026

This policy explains how the devtooligan Agent Messaging program handles information when you communicate with devtooligan-operated software and AI agents by SMS.

Information collected

The program may collect and process:

  • your mobile phone number;
  • your SMS opt-in status and consent records;
  • messages you send and responses delivered to you; and
  • delivery status, timestamps, and limited operational logs.

How information is used

This information is used to deliver requested messages, operate and secure the messaging program, respond to support requests, troubleshoot delivery, and comply with applicable law. Message contents may be processed by automated systems to provide the agent responses or notifications you request.

Mobile information and consent

Mobile information, SMS opt-in data, and messaging consent will not be sold, rented, or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Service providers may process mobile information only as needed to deliver messages, operate supporting infrastructure, prevent abuse, or provide technical support. They may not use it for their own marketing or promotional purposes.

Message frequency and charges

Message frequency varies based on user requests and agent activity. Message and data rates may apply according to your mobile carrier and plan.

Retention and security

Information is retained only as long as reasonably needed to operate and secure the program, resolve support or delivery issues, maintain consent records, and meet legal obligations. Reasonable safeguards are used to protect stored information, but no system is completely secure.

Your choices

Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages. Reply HELP for assistance. You may opt in again by texting START to the program number.

Contact

For privacy questions or support, visit github.com/devtooligan.
